1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT ABOUT? (THE CORE IDEA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When farmers apply chemical nitrogen fertilizer (such as Urea or DAP), two
major problems occur:
1. Wrong Dosage: Applying too much burns crops, damages soil, and wastes money.
   Applying too little starves the crop and lowers yield.
2. Bad Timing: Applying fertilizer before rain causes water to wash it deep into
   the ground (Leaching), which pollutes groundwater and wastes nutrients.
   Applying it on hot, dry, windy days causes chemical leaf burn (Burn Hazard).

GeoN-DSS solves this for 4 key districts of Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow, Kanpur,
Amethi, and Gorakhpur) by combining:
- Satellite vegetation indices (Sentinel-2 NDVI, GNDVI, NDRE)
- 48-hour weather forecasts (Rainfall, Temperature, Humidity, Wind)
- Soil properties from SoilGrids (Clay%, Sand%, Organic Carbon, pH)
- Agronomic details (Crop type, growth stage, days after sowing, previous crop)

Architecture Workflow:
[Farmer draws field on Map] -> [FastAPI Backend] -> [LightGBM Safety Classifier]
                                                            |
                 +------------------------------------------+--------------------+
                 |                                                               |
            [NO-GO Window]                                                  [GO Safe Window]
                 |                                                               |
     (Locks dosage; issues rain                                     (Unlocks XGBoost Regressor)
      or heat burn alert)                                                        |
                                                                    (Calculates exact kg N/ha,
                                                                     splits 40% now / 60% post-water,
                                                                     and counts 45kg Urea bags)


2. DATASET CREATION & PARAMETERS (64 COLUMNS, 15,000 ROWS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We created a 15,000-record physics-informed dataset based on Indian Council
of Agricultural Research (ICAR) regional fertilizer guidelines.

Key Parameter Groups:
- Geospatial: Field area (ha), coordinates, district.
- Satellite Spectral:
    * NDVI: Overall green plant canopy density.
    * GNDVI: Chlorophyll concentration.
    * NDRE: Red-Edge index; the most direct proxy for plant nitrogen health.
    * 15-Day Trends: Whether nitrogen health is improving or dropping.
- Weather: Max temp, avg temp, humidity, wind, 24h & 48h rain, and 48h forecast.
- Soil: Clay % (prevents leaching), Sand % (causes leaching), pH, organic carbon.
- Agronomic:
    * Crops: 15 UP crops (Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Maize, Potato, Mustard, Onion,
      Bajra, and Pulses like Arhar, Moong, Chickpea, Lentil, Soybean).
    * Seasons: Kharif (monsoon), Rabi (winter), Zaid (summer).
    * Previous Crop Credit: Growing pulses in the previous cycle leaves natural
      nitrogen in the soil, deducting 20 to 35 kg from the chemical requirement.


3. EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA) & WHAT THE FIGURES PROVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 10 figures are saved in the "ml/reports/figures/" folder:

- Figure 01 (Class Distribution):
    Shows 51.6% records are Leaching risk (due to heavy monsoon rain in UP Kharif),
    13.2% are Burn risk (hot dry summer days), and 35.2% are Safe GO windows.

- Figure 02 (Nitrogen Spread):
    Recommended nitrogen averages 24.6 kg N/ha, reaching up to 110 kg/ha for
    heavy-feeding crops like Sugarcane and Maize.

- Figure 03 (District & Season):
    Gorakhpur has heavy clay soil and high monsoon rain (high leaching risk).
    Kanpur has sandier soil which drains quickly.

- Figure 04 (NDRE vs. Nitrogen Demand):
    Proves that when NDRE is low or trending downwards, the crop is nitrogen
    deficient, so the recommended dosage increases.

- Figure 05 (Risk Score Verification):
    Confirms that Rain Leach Score > 4.0 and Burn Risk Score > 5.0 clearly
    distinguish safe days from risky days.

- Figure 06 (Nitrogen Demand by Crop):
    Ranked from highest to lowest: Sugarcane (50.5 kg/ha) > Maize (41.6 kg/ha) >
    Wheat (35.8 kg/ha). Legumes need very little (7-10 kg/ha) because they make
    their own nitrogen.

- Figure 07 (Growth Stage Demand):
    Nitrogen requirement peaks during Tillering and Booting (active stem and
    grain formation) and drops to zero at harvest maturity.

- Figure 08 (Spectral Indices):
    Shows how NDVI and NDRE rise as crops turn green and fall as crops mature.

- Figure 09 (Correlation Heatmap):
    Base crop demand has a strong positive correlation (+0.87) with recommendation.
    Previous pulse crop credit has a strong negative correlation (-0.38), saving

- Figure 10 (Weather States):
    Tracks frequency of sunny, cloudy, rainy, and foggy days across districts.


4. MACHINE LEARNING MODEL TRAINING & RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We trained two separate, specialized models:

Model 1: LightGBM Safety Classifier
- Task: 3-class classification (0: Leach Risk, 1: Burn Risk, 2: Safe Window).
- Balanced class weights were used to ensure minority burn-risk days are caught.
- Test Accuracy: 99.91%
- Macro F1-Score: 0.9985
- Confusion Matrix: Almost zero errors between classes.
- Saved File: ml/saved_models/lgbm_classifier.joblib

Model 2: XGBoost Dosage Regressor
- Task: Predict continuous nitrogen demand (kg N/ha).
- Evaluated on unseen test data.
- R-squared (R²): 0.9719 (explains over 97% of variance).
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 3.10 kg N/ha (prediction is within 3 kg of ideal).
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): 4.25 kg N/ha.
- Saved File: ml/saved_models/xgb_regressor.joblib


5. OTHER CRUCIAL OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Gatekeeper Interlock:
   If Model 1 predicts Class 0 or 1, the dosage is locked, and an alert is
   issued (e.g., "Heavy rain forecast; delay fertilizer to prevent leaching").
   Dosage is unlocked only when Model 1 confirms Class 2 (Safe).

2. Commercial Urea & DAP Bag Conversion:
   Neem-coated urea contains 46% Nitrogen. The system automatically converts
   kg N/ha into total Urea bags (45 kg each) and DAP bags (50 kg each) for the
   farmer's exact field acreage.

3. Split Application Schedule:
   To prevent nutrient shock and runoff, dosage is divided into:
   - 40% immediate application.
   - 60% post-irrigation application (applied after watering).

4. Leaflet Map Polygon Drawing:
   Farmers or field officers do not need GPS coordinates; they click corner points
   around the field on an interactive satellite map, which computes area automatically.

5. Full-Stack API Integration:
   - React + Vite on port 5173.
   - FastAPI on port 8000.
   - Real-time communication via REST endpoints.
